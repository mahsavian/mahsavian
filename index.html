The Narrative

I am a researcher and educator bridging 12 years of pedagogical practice with a scholarly focus on the "underground" origins of our digital world. My research is defined by a singular question: How does expertise survive the transition from informal, radical spaces to formal institutional structures?

My work is anchored in my Master’s research at the University of Padua, where I conducted a socio-technical autopsy of the pre-web digital underground. My thesis, "From BBS to Blackboard," utilized digital discourse analysis to map how Phrack Magazine functioned as an alternative university for a generation of hackers.
