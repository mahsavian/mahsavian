The Narrative

I am a researcher and educator bridging 12 years of pedagogical practice with a scholarly focus on the "underground" origins of our digital world. My research investigates how expertise survives the transition from informal, radical spaces to formal institutional structures.

Research & Digital Works

From BBS to Blackboard: The Pedagogy of the Exploit

STS Corpus Linguistics Voyant Tools

This research evaluates technical knowledge transfer through a longitudinal analysis of Phrack Magazine. Utilizing Voyant Tools, the study maps how technical identity is negotiated within decentralized networks. The analysis defines hacker rhetoric as a structured, high-stakes instructional system.

NAGPRA as a cultural encounter

STS Cultural Heritage Policy Analysis

An analytical review of Kathleen S. Fine-Dare's Grave Injustice. This paper explores the repatriation movement as a profound cultural encounter, examining how legal frameworks like NAGPRA intersect with the ongoing struggle for indigenous rights, cultural preservation, and historical justice.

Shapes of Educational Gender Discrimination in Iran

STS Gender Studies Education Policy

A critical analysis examining systemic educational gender discrimination in Iran. This research investigates disparities in educational access, bias within school textbooks, and sociocultural barriers imposed by traditional and political structures.
