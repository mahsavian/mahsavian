The Narrative

I am a researcher and educator bridging 12 years of pedagogical practice with a scholarly focus on the "underground" origins of our digital world. My work explores the Governance of Knowledge—investigating how expertise is negotiated, restricted, or reclaimed across informal digital spaces and formal state institutions.

Research & Geospatial Portfolio

From BBS to Blackboard: The Pedagogy of the Exploit

STS Corpus Linguistics Voyant Tools

This research evaluates the evolution of technical knowledge transfer through a longitudinal analysis of Phrack Magazine[cite: 247, 248]. Utilizing Voyant Tools for corpus linguistics, the study maps how technical identity and exploit methodologies are negotiated within decentralized networks. The analysis defines hacker rhetoric as a structured, high-stakes instructional system that governs the dissemination of specialized security intelligence.

Geospatial Analysis: Non-EU Residences in Italy

ArcGIS Big Data Visualization Demographics

Developed an interactive ArcGIS dashboard to visualize the distribution and legal status of non-EU residents across Italian regions. This project utilized proportional symbol mapping and big data visualization to analyze demographic trends in Lombardy and other key administrative areas in 2021.

Shapes of Educational Gender Discrimination in Iran

Critical Discourse Analysis Gender Studies

An investigation into institutionalized gender bias within the Iranian educational system[cite: 80, 88]. The research focuses on university admission quotas, where at least 30% of seats are often reserved for male students, and the reinforcement of patriarchal attitudes in high school textbooks[cite: 104, 124, 138]. The study also maps the legal barriers preventing women from entering specific professional fields, such as the judiciary or presidency[cite: 169, 170].

Grave Injustice: NAGPRA and Repatriation Ethics

Institutional Ethics Indigenous Rights

A comprehensive analysis of the Native American Graves Protection and Repatriation Act (NAGPRA)[cite: 246, 254]. The study explores the intersection of scientific authority and Indigenous sovereignty through high-profile case studies, such as the Kennewick Man, highlighting the ethical necessity of institutional commitment to cultural sensitivity and respect[cite: 254, 257, 307, 308].
