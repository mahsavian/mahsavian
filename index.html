The Narrative

I am a researcher and educator bridging 12 years of pedagogical practice with a scholarly focus on the "underground" origins of our digital world. My work explores the Governance of Knowledge—investigating how expertise is negotiated, restricted, or reclaimed across informal digital spaces and formal state institutions.

Research & Geospatial Portfolio

From BBS to Blackboard: The Pedagogy of the Exploit

STS Corpus Linguistics Voyant Tools

A longitudinal analysis of Phrack Magazine evaluating technical knowledge transfer within decentralized networks. This research uses data-driven linguistics to define hacker rhetoric as a structured, high-stakes instructional system.

Geospatial Analysis: Non-EU Residences in Italy

ArcGIS Big Data Visualization Demographics

Developed an interactive ArcGIS dashboard to visualize the distribution and legal status of non-EU residents across Italian regions. Utilized proportional symbol mapping and data visualization to analyze demographic trends in Lombardy and other key administrative areas.

Shapes of Educational Gender Discrimination in Iran

Critical Discourse Analysis Gender Studies

Investigated institutionalized gender bias within the Iranian educational system, focusing on university admission quotas, sexist textbook narratives, and legal barriers to professional advancement.

Grave Injustice: NAGPRA and Repatriation Ethics

Institutional Ethics Indigenous Rights

Analyzed the legal and ethical framework of the Native American Graves Protection and Repatriation Act (NAGPRA). Explored the intersection of scientific authority and indigenous sovereignty through high-profile case studies like the Kennewick Man.
